# v1.1 — Curated Practice Studio

Adds eight **individually authored, level-specific** supplementary learning modules to the existing 80 lesson cards. Each includes a contextual grammar note, Burmese explanation, four-turn dialogue with Burmese translations and browser speech, an American English expression, a listening check, a writing prompt with model answer, and speaking practice.

The supplementary module is shared by lessons at the same level. It is **not** 80 individually authored dialogue scripts. Existing lesson content and 24 world missions are preserved.

No AI service, authentication, or paid Firebase service is required. Progress and drafts remain local to the browser. Speech synthesis and speech recognition depend on browser support.
